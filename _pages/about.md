I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the [Min H. Kao Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science](https://www.eecs.utk.edu) at the [University of Tennessee, Knoxville](https://www.utk.edu). From October 2023 to July 2024 I was an MC2 Postdoctoral Fellow at the [Maryland Cybersecurity Center](https://cyber.umd.edu/about) at the University of Maryland, College Park. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the [University of Virginia](https://www.virginia.edu), advised by [David Evans](https://www.cs.virginia.edu/~evans/) and [Yuan Tian](https://www.ytian.info).

My research is on the **security of AI — not only the model, but the supporting infrastructure around it**.

**I am looking for self-motivated students** to work on trustworthy machine learning and machine learning for security. Please fill out the [questionnaire](https://forms.gle/fcx7c1CZX2mEEQwz9) and send me an email.

Suyee or Suya both work. I have not yet worked out how to persuade a passport office to print both.
